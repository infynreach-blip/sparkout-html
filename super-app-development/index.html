Super App Development Process

As a leading super app development company we provide a well structured and professional process for building your super apps. The process starts from the ideation stage of your project and extends to the deployment and launch of the app along with the long term maintenance and support.

1. Market Research & Ideation

The first step in building your super app is to understand the market and what your users want. We travel with you from the beginning stages of your journey to ideate a solution that aligns closely with your goals as a business and your target audience. In the very beginning we help you identify your target audience which includes the demographics, behaviors and preferences of your potential users. The next step is to analyze your competitors and see what existing super apps are capable of and possibly find gaps that we can fill to showcase your product as a unique solution to your users’ problems.

The next step in the process is to define the core features of your super app considering the research that we have already done the previous step and outline the essential functionalities that your users would need which can be popular services like payments, e-commerce and ride hailing. The next step is to figure out what the revenue model will look like according to what your needs are. You have quite a few options on this front like subscription plans, fees for transactions and selling spaces for advertising. Many super app based businesses generate significant revenue from advertising because many services like payments can align with the goals of your advertisers.

Last but definitely not the least is the compliance requirements that you may have to meet because depending on the country that you’re operating in you may have to research legal and regulatory guidelines which can include things like data privacy laws like GDPR or CCPA.

2. Strategic Planning & Roadmap Creation

As part of our end to end services we provide you with assistance in strategic planning and creating a solid roadmap for your project. The first step in this process is the feature prioritization where we help you define the features for your minimum viable product with the core features. For large scale projects that have already built an MVP for their product we can create a detailed roadmap and development plan and divide them into actionable sprints. The next step is to select the technologies that best suit your project which include the programming languages, frameworks and tools like React Native, Flutter, Node.js, AWS and so on.

The next step in the process is to create the foundation for your application through the architecture design. Here we can ensure that your application is scalable through the use of microservices or a modular architecture which also allows you to carry out easy maintenance of your application. The next stage is where all the magic happens where we allocate a dedicated product development team for you which includes developers, designers, test engineers, DevOps engineers and project managers.

3. UI/UX Design

During this stage your application will take shape and you will be able to see how it would look and feel on an actual device screen. The first step here is to ensure that the design philosophy is user centric and we create detailed wireframes and prototypes which focus on intuitive navigation for your users and provide them with user journeys that are seamless. On the user experience front we can leverage AI for things like personalized recommendations and dashboards where you can curate how your users experience your app.

The other major area of focus for us is about how responsive the design is and we design your app keeping in mind that a diverse set of users have to use your app depending on your target audience and the design has to work seamlessly across multiple devices like mobile, tablet and desktop. The final piece of the puzzle in user experience is including proper accessibility features into your application which can include features like voice commands and multilingual support so that your app reaches your target audience effectively.

4. Backend Development

This stage is where we build the heart of your super app and use the latest technologies, frameworks and tools to develop your backend. One of the major focuses here is the implementation of microservices where we break down functionalities into independent and scalable services for better handling which can include things like payments, chat and delivery modules. The database setup is a major part of this process where we help you choose the right database for your super app from the likes of MongoDB, MySQL or PostgreSQL for structured and unstructured data.

The next step in this process is the API development for your super app where we develop RESTful or GraphQL APIs so that your super app can seamlessly integrate with third party services like payment gateways and logistics software. The next and perhaps the most important step at this stage is the security integration for your super app which includes the implementation of encryption, secure authentication like OAuth 2.0 or biometrics and compliance with security standards.

5. Frontend Development

The next stage is the frontend development stage and it is crucial because your frontend is the first thing your users see when they open your app. This stage firstly includes the selection of the frameworks that we will use to build your super app like React Native, Flutter and so on. Depending on how your super app is designed we can choose between building a native app for each device or simplifying the process through cross platform app development with a framework like Flutter. Now we move on the real building of your frontend and create interactive and user friendly user interfaces for various features like chat, e-commerce, maps and payment gateways.

Any super app has to work in real time if you want your users to be able to use them everyday and for your app to provide an effective alternative to standalone apps. For this we can integrate solutions like WebSocket or Firebase for getting live updates like notifications or real time tracking. Another major aspect is the accessibility when not connected to the internet because you want to give your super app offline functionality also so that users can check in on their account even when they are not connected to a network. This involves the implementation of caching and offline modes for creating an uninterrupted experience for your users.

6 .Third-Party Integrations

One major part of any super app are the third party integrations that facilitate different functions within the app. Very important among these are the integration of payment gateways where we add secure payment solutions for your customers inside your super app like Stripe, PayPal or Razorpay. Another type of integration that could be very beneficial especially for tracking shipments and deliveries are the map and geolocation services. We can integrate APIs from platforms like Google Maps or Mapbox for location based services to function inside your super app and provide users with a seamless customer experience.

Authentication is another piece of making the onboarding seamless and in today’s day and age users often prefer to use social media logins to securely access different apps quickly. We can integrate social login options into your super app with tools like Google, Facebook or Apple ID login. The other major aspect is the push notifications so that you can communicate with your customers effectively and keep them informed. We use popular and trusted platforms like Firebase Cloud Messaging or OneSignal for effective push notifications.

7. Testing & Quality Assurance

The next stage is the testing and quality assurance so that we can ensure the products we build for you are secure, robust and serve the goals and needs of your business and your customers. This process starts with the unit testing of your product where we validate individual components for functionality. The next step is the integration testing where ensure that the communication between different modules and APIs of your super app are seamless. This step is followed by the performance testing of your super app where we test the app under various conditions that mimic real life usage of your app to ensure that it is stable. The next step would be the security testing where we conduct penetration testing to identify any vulnerabilities that your super app may have. Last but not the least is the user acceptance testing or UAT where we test your super app from a user’s point of view and refine the features for a better customer experience.

8. Deployment & Maintenance

The final stage of course is the deployment and then the continuous maintenance of your super app so that it performs well and gets the necessary updates as your business progresses. Here we deploy your super app on top cloud providers of your choosing like AWS, Azure and GCP depending on the nature and scale of your super app. We provide a free period of maintenance for bug fixes and minor changes and then you can opt for a paid annual maintenance contract or AMC if you want to continue getting maintenance and support services from us.
