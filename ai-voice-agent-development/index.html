What is an AI Voice Agent and Why Business Needs It?

An AI voice agent is an intelligent software system that takes advantage of advanced technologies, such as Natural Language Processing, speech recognition, and Machine Learning, to facilitate natural conversations with users. These agents can acknowledge voice commands given by humankind and engage in adaptive conversation to perform several tasks.

These AI voice agents can handle human tasks, including solving customer queries, solving real-world problems, and even scheduling appointments. By offering human-like conversations, voice agents can enhance the customer experience by reducing the manual workload.

How Does an AI Voice Agent Work in Real-Time?

To deal with a natural and human-like conversation, the AI voice agent must undergo a structured algorithm. From capturing the input to handling complex queries, a non-breakable flow should carry on to make things work. Here’s the detailed procedure for how the AI voice agent works in real-time.

01

Capturing Voice Input

The process starts with the AI voice agent hearing the user’s voice through the microphone. The initial layer filters the background noise and ensures the audio signal is clean.

02

Speech-to-Text Conversion

After the voice is being captured, the input is further converted into text using speech recognition. In this layer, the AI agent breaks down sounds and recognizes accents and tones.

03

Natural Language Understanding

The system further understands the converted text to identify the user’s intent and the meaning. This is the layer that bridges the gap between human speech and machine language.

04

Decision Making & Processing

As a next step in the algorithm, the agent determines the right action or response for the given input. The system will fetch data from the database and ensure relevant results will be delivered.

05

Text-to-Speech Response

Upon processing, the system converts the text result into natural human-like speech. The advanced TTS engines ensure the response is clear, natural, and error-free for seamless conversations.

06

Continuous Learning & Feedback

Unlike others, AI voice agents improve themselves with every interaction they make. Regular fine-tuning and training help the AI voice agent to handle customer queries quickly.
