Understanding Software Scalability and Its Types

Software scalability is nothing but the system’s ability to hold an increasing workload or expand its capacity without sacrificing the quality. In today’s digital environment, scalable software ensures that applications can expand with the growing demand and data volume, and provide a seamless experience for users and businesses.

There are different ways to scale a software, and each addresses different aspects of growth. Speaking of its types, ‘vertical scalability’ involves adding more resources to existing hardware, ‘horizontal scalability’ adds more nodes to distribute the load, and ‘diagonal scalability’ combines both approaches to scale flexibly based on current requirements.
