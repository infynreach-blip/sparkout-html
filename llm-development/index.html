What is LLM Development and
What’s Its Role in Business Development?

LLM development is an in-depth process of designing, building, deploying, and enhancing Large Language Models (LLMs) that can understand and act according to human-like text. These text generation models are trained on huge amounts of data to understand and adapt to human content and patterns.

The AI development team will later fine-tune these models and optimize them for performing tasks, including coding, debugging, and customer care. Later, the LLM models are deployed into custom applications, chatbots, and AI tools for real-time use.
