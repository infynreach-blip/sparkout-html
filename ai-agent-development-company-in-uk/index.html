Key AI Agent Types Every UK Business Should Know

If your business needs AI agent development for sustainable growth, understanding the different types that we offer is crucial. Only then you can choose the right architecture and service offerings. Here are the key categories of AI agents that we deliver and their relevance for UK businesses and enterprises.

Simple Reflex Agents

We built this type of agent to respond to immediate inputs using a predefined rule-based algorithm without internal memory or planning.

Model-Base Reflex Agents

These agents help businesses to assemble and maintain an internal model of the environment and deal with partially observable conditions.

Goal-Based Agents

With predictive analysis, these agents can evaluate the future states and do plan sequences of actions to meet specific goals within a time.

Learning Agents

Compared to others, they kind of agents evolve over time, learning from past experience, and adjust their behaviour to boost performance.

Utility-Based Agents

Apart from simply aiming for the goal, utility-based agents evaluate different choices to maximize the utility function for businesses.

Hierarchial Agents

These types of AI agents organize their decision-making in different layers to improve scalability, especially for complicated operations.
